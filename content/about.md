## Key Milestones

### 2012 — My First Computer

At 15 years old, I had the opportunity to get a computer for the first time. It was a discontinued model from a bank that I received as a gift — an already old and abandoned machine with poor hardware specifications.

While setting up the PC, I immediately noticed a start menu that appeared after inserting a CD. I wanted to create something like that myself and searched for ways to make it happen — the foundation for my entry into the world of programming.

### 2013 — Small Tools

- First steps with C#, PHP, JavaScript, and HTML to develop my own applications.
- Recreation of applications like a URL shortener, a custom CMS, and a browser replica.
- Development of a plugin for Minecraft as an entry into game programming.
- Deepening knowledge through continuous experimentation and optimization.

### 2015 — First Successes with "Real" Projects

- Gained practical experience through part-time work at Sturmfabrik.
- Independently developed a website for the Youth City Council as a member.
- Completed first professional backend projects for companies like Paisen Anhänger.
- Focus on WordPress and WooCommerce projects to expand web competencies.

### 2016 — Start of My Apprenticeship

- Completed internships in software development, automotive mechatronics, and electrical engineering.
- Early decision for IT due to great enthusiasm for programming.
- Started apprenticeship at SSI Schäfer IT Solutions with focus on SAP development.
- Deepened backend technologies and completed first projects in a corporate environment.

### 2019 — Apprenticeship Completed & Hired

- Successfully completed apprenticeship as software developer at SSI Schäfer.
- Direct hire and permanent employment for further specialization in SAP for customer projects and standard development.
- Focus on EWM, MFS, and Web Dynpro for optimization of warehouse logistics systems.
- Started with first independent software solutions for internal and external projects.

### 2020 — Founded My Own Business

- Started self-employment with focus on individual software and web development.
- Specialization in digital business processes, automation, and solutions.
- Built a client network and successfully completed first projects.
- Established high quality standards for sustainable software solutions by building a tech stack based on years of practical experience.

### 2021 — SWAN GmbH

- Transition to SWAN GmbH as part of the integration with the SSI Schäfer Group.
- Focus on frontend technologies like SAP UI5, Fiori, and backend processes with ABAP and ABAP RAP (SAP Cloud / SAP BTP).
- Introduction of modern technologies like TypeScript, ESLint, and automated tests to simplify development and ensure quality standards.
- Development of plugins and extensions to reduce development costs and increase efficiency.

### 2023 — Artificial Intelligence

- With the release of ChatGPT, deepened knowledge in AI technologies for optimizing business processes.
- Successfully automated first projects through AI-powered solutions like ChatGPT, using the OpenAI API.
- In-depth topics like prompt engineering to avoid high AI usage costs and improve the quality of results.
- Continuous research of new AI standards, alternatives, and possibilities to always offer the best possible solutions.

### 2024 — Product Development & Training

- Started product development at SWAN GmbH for innovative software solutions.
- Implementation of a first product successfully deployed at customer sites.
- Successfully obtained trainer certification for developing new talent.
- Mentoring and professional guidance of apprentices.
- Conducting internal training sessions for employees.
